# Final image-generation prompt set

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the original pickup-board image as a visual reference.

1. **Splash:** 16:9 cartoon claw-game splash titled `CLAW GAME`; centered claw grabbing a blue spherical plush; capsules, coin, bomb, magnet, and crystal prizes; deep navy/cyan/purple/red/gold palette; bold outlines and solid-color shapes; no photographic textures.
2. **Static button:** blank reusable cyan arcade button with deep-navy frame and gold corners on transparent background.
3. **Hover button:** preserve the static silhouette; brighten cyan/gold and add a thin white-cyan inner outline; transparent background.
4. **Pressed button:** preserve the static silhouette; dark royal-blue inset face with depressed-state feedback; transparent background.
5. **Arrows:** matched left/right white arrows on cyan rounded-diamond plates with navy outlines and gold accents; transparent atlas.
6. **Slider:** long navy/cyan/gold track plus separate normal and hover claw-token knobs; transparent atlas.
7. **Progress bars:** segmented gold-orange-red combo bar with star cap and cyan-blue round-timer bar with clock cap; no words or numbers; transparent atlas.
8. **Empty combo frame:** preserve the navy/blue frame and fixed star medallion; remove fill and moving marker; transparent background.
9. **Combo fill:** isolated rounded segmented yellow-orange-red fill only; no frame or icons; transparent background.
10. **Empty timer frame:** preserve the navy/blue frame and fixed clock medallion; remove fill and moving marker; transparent background.
11. **Timer fill:** isolated rounded segmented cyan-to-blue fill only; no frame or icons; transparent background.
12. **Tooltip:** blank wide navy/blue hover panel with gold corners and a centered downward pointer; clean interior for runtime text; transparent background.

The hover, pressed, and empty-timer outputs received final background-extraction edits to replace accidentally baked checkerboards with genuine alpha transparency.
